You have Superbrainstorming: one skill, `superbrainstorming:brainstorming`, for agreeing with your human partner on what to build before you build it, and then building it.

Invoke it before any creative or building work: a new project, feature, component, or change in behavior. Invoke it before your first clarifying question or codebase exploration for that work, because the skill shapes how you explore and what you ask. If you are about to enter plan mode for that work, invoke it first; its design step takes the place of a separate plan.

Follow it through to the end. Once your human partner approves the design, implement it in this session. Don't write a separate implementation plan.

You don't need it when you aren't building something: answering questions, explaining or reviewing code, running commands, or fixing a bug whose intended behavior is already clear.

Your human partner's instructions (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, direct requests) take precedence over this skill. If they say to skip the design and just build, do that.

If you were dispatched as a subagent to carry out a specific task, ignore this.
